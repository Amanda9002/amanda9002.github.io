✨ What is peekie?

peekie is a simple, beautiful scratchpad that lives in the menu bar of your Mac. Whenever an idea hits you, press the shortcut and a small, translucent window appears instantly. Write your note, close it, and get back to what you were doing. No clutter, no distractions, just your thoughts.

It's built natively with Swift, which means it's fast, lightweight, and feels right at home on macOS. There are no accounts to create, no cloud syncing, and no network access—your notes stay on your computer, private and secure.

🎯 Why Use peekie?

  • Instant Capture: Your idea is fleeting. With one shortcut, you're already writing. No searching for an app, no opening a blank document.
  • Minimal Design: The translucent window floats over your screen without blocking your view. Write, close, done.
  • Privacy First: No accounts, no servers, no cloud. Your notes are yours alone.
  • Native Speed: Built with Swift and AppKit, it's quick to open, quick to close, and takes up almost no memory.
  • Keyboard Friendly: Everything can be done with the keyboard. If you're a fast typer, you'll never touch the mouse.
  • Open Source: The code is open and available for anyone to review or contribute. Transparency built-in.

🎨 How It Works

Using peekie is almost second nature. Here's the usual flow:

  1. Summon: Press your chosen shortcut (e.g., Option+Space). A small, translucent window fades in at the center of your screen.
  2. Write: Type whatever comes to mind—a phone number, a quick reminder, an idea for a story, a grocery item.
  3. Dismiss: Press the shortcut again or click elsewhere. The window disappears. Your note is saved automatically.
  4. Retrieve: When you need to see your notes, just open the menu bar icon and browse through your recent scratchpads.

It's a beautiful, frictionless loop that keeps you in your flow, whether you're working, reading, or on a call.

🔧 Frequently Asked Questions

No. peekie has absolutely no network access. It cannot send or receive data. Your notes are stored locally on your Mac.

Can I change the shortcut?

Yes. In the preferences, you can assign any key combination you like to summon and hide the scratchpad.
